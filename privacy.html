Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • OAuth Tokens: Access tokens provided by TikTok's OAuth flow
  • Video Content: Video files you choose to upload for publishing
  • Analytics Data: Performance metrics retrieved from your account

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information solely to publish videos as directed by you, retrieve and display analytics data, and maintain Service functionality.

3. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device. We do not maintain external servers containing your personal information.

4. Third-Party Services

The Service integrates with TikTok's official API. We do not share your data with any other third parties.

5. Your Rights

  • Revoke OAuth access at any time through your TikTok account settings
  • Delete all locally stored data by uninstalling the application

6. Security

We implement reasonable security measures including encrypted token storage and secure API communication over HTTPS.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact us through the application.
